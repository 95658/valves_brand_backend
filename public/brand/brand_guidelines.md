# VALVES — Complete Brand Identity Guidelines (2026)

## 1. Brand Essence & Manifesto
> **"Control the flow. Break the resistance."**

**VALVES** is a contemporary hybrid apparel label bridging **High-End Streetwear**, **Minimalist Casual Basics**, and **High-Performance Athleisure**.

The name derives from the physical mechanism that regulates pressure, flow, and energy. In apparel, **VALVES** represents:
* **The Flow:** Fluidity of motion, effortless oversized draping, and seasonal drops.
* **The Resistance:** Heavyweight fabrics (380-460 GSM french terry & fleece), reinforced stitching, weatherproof tech membranes.
* **The Release:** Breaking everyday monotony through progressive cuts and functional aesthetics.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Primary Palette (The Core)
* **Obsidian Black (`#09090b` / `black`):** Base grounding color for streetwear silhouettes, dark UI, and luxury garment boxes.
* **Platinum Frost (`#f8fafc` / `white`):** High contrast typography, light garment colorways, and minimal web canvas.
* **Titanium Gray (`#27272a` / `#71717a`):** Secondary borders, technical specs, metal zippers, and hardware.

### Signature Accents (The Voltage)
* **Cyber Volt (`#d4ff00`):** Used sparingly as a high-visibility kinetic spark (1% rule: embroidery bar tacks, pull-tabs, app badge accents, status highlights).
* **Thermal Crimson (`#ff334b`):** Limited drop alerts, active state indicators, and technical heat-seal stamps.

---

## 3. Typography Architecture

### Display & Wordmarks
* **Primary Display:** `Space Grotesk` / `Neue Haas Grotesk` (Extended tracking: `letter-spacing: 0.15em` to `0.25em`).
* **Characteristics:** Monospaced geometric undertones, sharp apexes, technical precision.

### UI & Body Copy
* **Primary Body:** `Inter` / `Geist Sans` / `SF Pro Display`.
* **Weights:** Regular (400) for product descriptions, Medium (500) for navigation, Bold (700) for prices and callouts.

### Clear Space Rule
Maintain minimum padding equal to **height of the letter 'V'** on all sides of the wordmark and monogram. Never stretch, warp, or apply drop shadows directly to the vector contours.
